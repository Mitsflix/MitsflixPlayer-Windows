- **Clearer error messages.** When something goes wrong, the app now says what happened and what to do, in plain English. For example: your username or password isn't right, the server address can't be found, the server is down for a few minutes, your internet provider may be blocking it, your playlist is already playing on another device, or your subscription has ended.
- Tips point you to your backup or VPN server address when that's likely to fix it.
